# Iron Temple Tycoon

A gym-owner life sim that runs in the browser. You start at 22 with $25, a couch in the back office and about 200 days to live. Coach clients, take side hustles, earn fitness certifications, trade supplies, build credit and grow a garage gym into a global fitness empire before your time runs out.

## How to play

- **Every action takes one day.** Coach clients, rest, do a hustle or train yourself, and a day passes: dues come in, bills go out and you get a day older. Nothing happens while the game is closed.
- **Stay alive.** Better housing, better meals, certifications, training and checkups add days to your life. Running out of cash costs you extra days.
- **Build the gym.** Every piece of equipment you buy shows up on the floor, and members walk over and use it. Tap **Rearrange** to drag things where you want them.
- **When you die,** your best net worth becomes legacy stars. Each star adds 10% to everything you earn in your next life.

## Play on iPhone

Open `index.html` from any web host (for example GitHub Pages), then in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen like an app. Progress is saved in the browser on that device.
